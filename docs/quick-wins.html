How to review

  • "Today" is rebuilt from what we saw on the live site; "Proposed" is the change. Everything else is kept the same, so the difference is the change itself.
  • Use Quick win in the bar at the top to jump to any of them, or the previous and next links at the foot of each page.
  • Switch Notes on to see what each quick win proposes and why, with notes on behaviour and open questions for Tideway.
  • 10 of these also appear inside the five big improvements. Here each one stands alone, so they can be chosen separately.
