Prototype 3 of 5 · What's happening near me · Site page

What we're proposing

Site pages that open with where things stand: status, when the page was last updated, what's next and who to contact. Below that, dated notices you can filter, a sign-up for updates about this site, the liaison group record as a table and the documents with readable titles.

Why

Chambers Wharf today has no last-updated date, a works update written in 2025, liaison meetings listed in one long sentence (saying the last meeting was on 16 December 2025, then linking notes for the 15th) and seven planning documents named like file names in capitals. Part way down, the page also prints a server error. The content residents need is there; it's the order and the upkeep that let it down.

Shown with Chambers Wharf; opens any site with ?site=. Notices, status and next steps are sample data. Meeting dates are from the live page.
