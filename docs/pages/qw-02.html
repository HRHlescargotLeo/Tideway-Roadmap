Quick win 2 of 20 · Task links on the homepage

What we're proposing

A row of six task links straight under the hero: see the river results, visit a riverside space, check what's happening near me, report a problem, find a document and investors. Nothing else on the homepage needs to change.

Why

The only task links on the homepage today are at the very foot, under the map and the modern slavery statement: Contact us, Find a document and What's happening near me?

Also part of big improvement 1. It can be made on its own, without the rest of that improvement.
