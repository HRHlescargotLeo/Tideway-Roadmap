Prototype 3 of 5 · What's happening near me · Contact

What we're proposing

A contact form that asks what the enquiry is about before anything else, sends it to the right team (or to Thames Water, with their details, if it's theirs now), asks which site and postcode, lets people add a photo, and finishes with a reference number and a clear promise of when they'll hear back.

Why

The live form has four inputs: name, email, message and a Yes/No consent choice. It doesn't ask what the enquiry is about or where, so every message arrives in one pile and someone has to read it to route it. The reply times residents are promised (five working days, ten at most) are on Help & Advice, not on the form they've just filled in.

Opens with a topic and site already chosen from other pages, for example ?topic=works&site=chambers-wharf. The complaint acknowledgement time is a placeholder.
