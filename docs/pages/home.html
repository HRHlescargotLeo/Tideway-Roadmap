Prototype 1 of 5 · It's working

What we're proposing

A homepage for the years the tunnel is running rather than the years it was being built. It opens with what the tunnel has done for the river, puts the jobs people come to do within one click, explains plainly who now looks after what, and uses a navigation regrouped around river health, visiting, the tunnel and neighbours.

Why

The first Benefits Report found overflow volumes to the tidal Thames down about 86% in 2025, and every new public space is open. None of that is on the homepage, which leads with a video and a counter whose HTML reads "0 million tonnes" until it animates. The rest of the site still describes construction in the present tense: About Us expects completion in 2025 and Corporate Info in 2024. With operation passing to Thames Water, people will also need telling who to call, and at the moment nothing does.

Figures are from Tideway's Benefits Report (12 Oct 2026) and the live counter. Contact routes marked [to confirm] need agreeing with Tideway and Thames Water.

London's super sewer

The super sewer is working. Here's what it has done for the Thames.

In its first full year, the London Tideway Tunnel cut the sewage overflowing into the tidal Thames by about 86%. Operation is now passing to Thames Water; Tideway continues to own and maintain the tunnel.
