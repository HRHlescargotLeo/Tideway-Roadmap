Quick win 9 of 20 · Readable document names

What we're proposing

List site documents by a plain title, with the document type, format, size and date, across all site pages.

Why

Chambers Wharf lists its seven planning documents as file names in capitals, such as “CODE OF CONSTRUCTION PRACTICE-PART A_CHAMBERS WHARF.PDF”, with no size or date.

Also part of big improvement 3. It can be made on its own, without the rest of that improvement.

Quick win 9

Readable document names

Plain titles with type, size and date, instead of file names in capitals.
