Prototype 1 of 5 · It's working · Who does what now

What we're proposing

One page that explains the handover in plain terms: what Tideway still does, what Thames Water now does, and who to contact for each kind of problem, with the right number shown as soon as someone picks their situation.

Why

The Benefits Report story says operation of the tunnel "is transferred to Thames Water from Tideway". About Us says Thames Water will operate it and Tideway will maintain it. Neither tells a resident who smells something near a shaft, or sees a discharge, which number to ring. The helpdesk will otherwise field calls it can no longer act on.

Who handles each situation is our assumption and needs agreeing with Tideway and Thames Water. Numbers shown are Tideway's published helpdesk and the Environment Agency incident hotline; others are marked [to confirm].

Who looks after what now

The tunnel is finished and working. Thames Water now runs it day to day. Tideway still owns it, maintains it and is completing work at a few sites.

The two roles

Tideway

Owns and maintains the tunnel

  • Finishing works and landscaping at the last sites
  • Construction compensation and complaints
  • The Benefits Report and river results
  • Investors, bondholders and the press
Thames Water

Operates the tunnel day to day

  • Running the tunnel and its pumping
  • Odour from shafts and ventilation
  • Live storm overflow status
  • Sewers and drains connected to the tunnel

Open questionIs this split right? In particular, who handles odour from Tideway ventilation columns, and who maintains the new public spaces?

What do you need help with?

BehaviourChoosing a situation shows who handles it, why, how to reach them and how long a reply takes. Links can be shared with the situation preselected, for example from a site page (?issue=works).
