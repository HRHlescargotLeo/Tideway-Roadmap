Quick win 1 of 20 · Who does what now

What we're proposing

A short page explaining the handover: what Tideway still does, what Thames Water now does, and who to contact for each kind of problem, with the right number shown as soon as someone picks their situation. Linked from Contact and the footer.

Why

The Benefits Report story says operation of the tunnel “is transferred to Thames Water from Tideway”. About Us says Thames Water will operate the tunnel and Tideway will maintain it. Neither tells a resident who smells something near a shaft which number to ring, so the helpdesk will field calls it can no longer act on.

Also part of big improvement 1. It can be made on its own, without the rest of that improvement.
