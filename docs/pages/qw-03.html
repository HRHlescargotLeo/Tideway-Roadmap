Quick win 3 of 20 · Search snippets that describe today

What we're proposing

Rewrite page titles and meta descriptions for the operating years, and give each section a photographic share image served over https, so the site reads correctly in search results and when a link is shared.

Why

The homepage description in search results is “Tideway is building a 25km Super Sewer under the Thames to intercept those nasty spills…”. The share image is the logo, served over http.

Also part of big improvement 1. It can be made on its own, without the rest of that improvement.
