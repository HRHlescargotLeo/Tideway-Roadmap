Quick win 6 of 20 · How we count this, in plain English

What we're proposing

A “How we count this” note beside the counter: what's measured, how often it's updated, what it doesn't include and who checks it, in plain English and at a readable size.

Why

The counter's only explanation is an asterisked footnote at 13px, white on the sea-green panel, which is 3.14:1 and below the contrast needed for small text.

Also part of big improvement 2. It can be made on its own, without the rest of that improvement.

Quick win 6

How we count this, in plain English

A short explanation beside the counter, instead of a 13px footnote.

Try: Open “How we count this” under the proposed counter.

Today

22.2 million tonnes

of sewage diverted from the River Thames*

*into the London Tideway Tunnel system since August 2024. The tunnel was fully connected and operational in February, 2025. Data last updated 1/10/2026.

Proposed

22.2 million tonnes

of sewage diverted from the River Thames since it was connected

What it measures. The volume of combined sewage and rainwater diverted into the tunnel system instead of spilling into the river. One tonne is about one cubic metre.

How often. Updated once a month, on the first working day.

What it leaves out. Overflows not connected to the tunnel, and the non-tidal river upstream.

Who checks it. [Method and validation to confirm with Tideway.]
