Prototype 3 of 5 · What's happening near me

What we're proposing

A postcode lookup that lists Tideway's 24 sites nearest first, says plainly whether each one still has works, is now a public space or is simply complete, and leads to a site page with dated notices, update alerts and a contact form that sends the enquiry to the right people.

Why

The live lookup took the postcode SE16 4ND, showed "Your Location: SE1 9PX" and left the sites in west-to-east order, so the nearest wasn't obvious. Site pages are written for the construction years: Chambers Wharf's works update refers to Summer 2024 and Spring 2025, and residents are asked to email an address to get updates. Fewer sites now have works, which makes it more important, not less, that the ones that do are easy to find and easy to follow.

Site names and boroughs are from tideway.london. Statuses are sample data. Postcodes are matched by district in this prototype; a live build would use a postcode service.
