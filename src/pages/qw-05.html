Quick win 5 of 20 · The real number in the counter

What we're proposing

Print the current figure in the page HTML and count up only when the counter scrolls into view and the visitor hasn't asked for reduced motion.

Why

The counter's HTML reads “0 million tonnes” until a script animates it to 22.2 when it scrolls into view. Link previews, search engines and some screen readers can be left with the zero.

Also part of big improvement 1. It can be made on its own, without the rest of that improvement.
