Prototype 5 of 5 · Find a document

What we're proposing

One search across pages, news, documents and video, with a summary, type, date and size on every result and filters for type, topic, site and year. Alongside it, annual reports listed newest first, a short investor panel and the learning legacy material grouped by theme.

Why

Searching the live site for "noise" returns 58 results four at a time, across 15 pages, as titles only; one of them is a raw file name. The Document Library is twenty-odd category links with no search, dates or sizes. Its annual reports run 2019-20, then back to 2015-16, then 2020-21 to 2022-23, and stop there, although the 2025-26 report was published in June. For a company whose work is now largely in the record, finding that record should be the easy part.

Titles of news, reports and videos are real. Summaries, file sizes and some dates are sample data.
