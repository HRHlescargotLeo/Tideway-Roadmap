Quick win 17 of 20 · Teal text that passes contrast

What we're proposing

Use a deeper teal, #00796A (5.33:1 on white), for teal text below 19px bold and anywhere on grey. Keep Tideway teal for large headings, buttons and fills, where it already passes.

Why

Tideway teal, #00A88F, is 3.0:1 on white and 2.75:1 on the light grey. That is fine for the 22px Cubano headings but fails for smaller teal labels and links.
