Quick win 4 of 20 · One completion date, told the same way everywhere

What we're proposing

Agree one sequence of milestones (connected, capturing, fully operational, last riverside space opens, handover) and show it as the same strip on every page that talks about completion.

Why

Three pages give three answers. The homepage counter footnote says the tunnel was “fully connected and operational in February, 2025” and counts from August 2024. The Tunnel page says the network was completed in May 2024, and also that “the system commissioning phase has now started”.

Also part of big improvement 1. It can be made on its own, without the rest of that improvement.
