Quick win 12 of 20 · Bring About Us up to date

What we're proposing

Rewrite About Us for the operating years and check every figure with Tideway. The facts then live in one place that About Us, Corporate Info and the press pages all use.

Why

About Us still says “construction is now underway at each of our 24 sites” and that the project is “anticipated to be completed in 2025”. It says “more than two million pensioners” and later “Three million pensioners”, and that Thames Water supplies “around 2.6 million litres of tap water” a day, where Thames Water's own figure is 2.6 billion.
