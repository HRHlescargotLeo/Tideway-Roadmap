Quick win 8 of 20 · A liaison group record you can scan

What we're proposing

Show each Community Liaison Working Group as a short table: meeting date, notes and slides, newest first, with the next meeting date (or a line saying the group has finished) at the top.

Why

On Chambers Wharf the record is one long run of dates and links. It opens “The last meeting was on 16 December 2025” and then lists “15 December 2025 presentation and notes”.

Also part of big improvement 3. It can be made on its own, without the rest of that improvement.
