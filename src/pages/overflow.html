What we're proposing

A page for each place the tunnel connects to London's sewers, showing what the tunnel has caught there month by month, and linking to Thames Water's live status for the same overflow.

Why

The Tunnel page steps through 21 overflows on a map but says nothing about any of them beyond the name. People who live by one, or row past it, want to know whether it still spills. Answering that per location is more convincing than any city-wide total.

All figures on this page are sample data. Opens any connection with ?site=, for example ?site=bazalgette-embankment.
