Five ideas for tideway.london

The tunnel is built and working, and the site should say so. These clickable prototypes show five improvements for the years ahead: leading with the results, proving them, keeping neighbours informed, welcoming people to the new riverside and making the record easy to find.

The prototypes

  1. It's working
    1

    A homepage that says it's working

    The river results up front, a counter with the real figure in it, the jobs people come to do within one click, a navigation for the operating years, and a plain explanation of who now looks after what.

    Try: open the River health menu at the top, then pick “There's a smell near a Tideway site” under Who looks after what now.

    Open prototype
  2. River health
    2

    The Benefits Report as a living page

    The four objectives before and after, a step-through of what happens when it rains, a page for each overflow connection, a monthly update and how the figures are counted.

    Try: step through “What happens when it rains”, then filter the overflows to East and open the one that caught most.

    Open prototype
  3. Near me
    3

    What's happening near me

    A postcode lookup sorted by distance, site pages with a status panel and dated notices, update alerts, and a contact form that sends each enquiry to the right people.

    Try: enter SE16 4ND, open Chambers Wharf, sign up for updates, then report a problem there.

    Open prototype
  4. Visit the riverside
    4

    Visit the new riverside

    A guide to the seven new public spaces, a visitor page for each, Active Tideway sessions and other events, an art trail and the Tideway Prize.

    Try: filter to spaces that flood at high tide, open King Edward Memorial Park Foreshore, then filter What's on to Active Tideway.

    Open prototype
  5. Find a document
    5

    Find any document

    One search across pages, news, documents and video, with summaries and filters, annual reports in order, an investor panel and the learning legacy.

    Try: search “Chambers Wharf”, tick Planning documents, then sort by newest.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • These use Tideway's own design system and photography, so they show how each idea would look on tideway.london. Fonts are close stand-ins for Tideway's licensed typefaces.
  • Everything interactive works: menus, the who-to-contact chooser, filters, the postcode lookup, map pins, sign-ups, the contact steps and search. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Tideway (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Site names, boroughs, published figures and news titles are Tideway's. Statuses, notices, monthly figures, per-site figures, events and file sizes are sample data.
